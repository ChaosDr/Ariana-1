AN IMMERSIVE CASE STUDY EXERCISE

The Composed Hour: Ariana

You are the therapist. Ariana, impeccably dressed and entirely composed, arrives on time and gives you exactly what you ask for, no more. Beneath the flatness is a decade of medication, a twin she can't outrun, and a pattern with partners that puzzles everyone around her.

Two gauges track her: Detachment, how far she retreats into flatness, politeness, or her phone, and Trust, how safe she feels letting anything real surface. Push past what she can hold and she checks out entirely. Stay too gentle, and the hour stays polished and empty. There is no perfect script.

Reading your trace

  • Where did detachment climb fastest, and what was being approached right before it did?
  • Which of your choices got a polished, agreeable answer that changed nothing? What did that politeness protect her from?
  • What did you feel during the pause, and what might that tell you about what it's like to be close to her?

Take it to your group

Before anything else: choose one detail from Ariana's story that you find important or puzzling, and invite each person in your group to share theirs.

Your task: build an interpretation of who Ariana is and what the flatness might be protecting. Several interpretations may fit, so use details from the case to explain yours. Start with a question that interests your group. You don't need to finish every question or agree with each other.

  • Who is Ariana, actually? Strip away the twin sister, the father's money, the diagnoses, the ex-boyfriends. If none of that existed, what would be left? How hard is it to build a picture of her from the inside, based only on what she values, wants, or feels, and what does that difficulty itself tell you?
  • She's spent her whole life next to someone who is, by the family's accounting, the successful version of her: not an older sibling, a twin. Same starting point, constant comparison. Before analyzing anything, what do you imagine that actually feels like, day to day, over ten or fifteen years?
  • “Emotionally stunted” or emotionally defended? Is the flatness the real Ariana, or is it doing something for her? What might it be protecting, and from what?
  • She performs, but for whom? Impeccably dressed, polite, composed, present but rarely fully there. Is the gap between how she presents and who she is inside unusually large, or just unusually visible? Do you recognize anything of yourself in this?
  • She left a fiancé over mild marijuana use, then days later was with someone dealing with serious addiction who was sometimes verbally abusive. Without judging, construct a theory for why this makes psychological sense from the inside. What might she be getting, or avoiding, in each choice?
  • If you were someone in her life, what would you actually try? What do you think is the real work here, and is there anything in her situation that deserves more compassion than she typically receives?

When you have an initial answer, stay with it a little longer:

  • What in the story makes us think that?
  • What else could explain the same behavior?
  • What would we need to know to tell the difference?

For example, “she's just cold” is a starting point. Is she avoiding vulnerability, has she learned that feeling doesn't get her anywhere, does chronic boredom stand in for something harder to name, or something else? Her chart also lists a formal social anxiety diagnosis that doesn't match how comfortably she attends gatherings, so it's worth asking what else could be happening, and what you'd need to know to tell the difference.

Changing your mind, questioning an interpretation, and noticing missing information all count as contributions. You don't need to share personal experiences. Please discuss without AI. No written answers are required.
